\## 15.8 Reference Usage in the Project



The references were used for different components of the project.



\### Clinical Data



The UCL NCL Mutation and Patient Database provided the structured clinical and mutation data used for CLN1, CLN2, and CLN3 analysis.



\### MRI Research



Open-access research publications on neuronal ceroid lipofuscinoses and Batten disease provided the scientific background and MRI image source material used in the MRI dataset preparation.



\### Deep Learning



EfficientNet research provided the basis for the transfer-learning architecture used in the MRI classifier.



\### Explainable AI



Grad-CAM provided the methodological basis for the MRI attention visualization.



\### Machine Learning



Scikit-learn and Random Forest references supported the implementation of the structured clinical machine learning component.



\### Application Development



Python, TensorFlow/Keras, OpenCV, Pillow, Joblib, NumPy, Pandas, and Streamlit documentation supported the implementation of the complete research application.

\## 15.9 Important Reference and Data Disclaimer



The referenced clinical datasets and research publications were used for academic and research purposes.



The MRI images obtained from research publications are not equivalent to a standardized clinical imaging dataset.



The project does not claim ownership of the underlying published medical research or clinical data.



All external datasets, publications, software libraries, and research methods should be appropriately acknowledged when presenting or distributing the project.



The developed system is an academic research prototype and is not a clinically validated diagnostic system.
