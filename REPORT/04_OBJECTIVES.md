\# 3. OBJECTIVES



\## 3.1 Primary Objective



The primary objective of this project is to develop an Artificial Intelligence-based research prototype for analyzing Batten Disease-related clinical information and brain MRI images using machine learning, deep learning, and explainable AI techniques.



---



\## 3.2 Specific Objectives



\### Objective 1 — Process Clinical Data



To collect, clean, preprocess, and organize clinical and mutation information associated with CLN1, CLN2, and CLN3.



---



\### Objective 2 — Develop a Clinical Machine Learning Model



To evaluate supervised machine learning algorithms for predicting NCL subtypes from processed clinical information.



The evaluated algorithms include:



\- Logistic Regression

\- Decision Tree

\- Random Forest



Random Forest is selected as the final clinical model based on its overall validation performance.
